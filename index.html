binger barcode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-03

binger barcode ("the app") is an unofficial utility that displays your recreation center entry barcode. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

binger barcode does not operate any servers of its own and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information with the developer or any third party. All authentication happens directly between your device and your school's existing recreation/identity provider.

What the app stores

  • Session token. When you log in, the app receives a session token from your school's recreation system. This token is stored only on your device, in encrypted storage (Android Jetpack Security / EncryptedSharedPreferences). It is used solely to request your current entry barcode.
  • The app does not store your username or password. Login credentials are entered into your school's own web login page shown inside the app and are handled by that provider, not by binger barcode.

What the app transmits

  • The app sends your session token to your school's recreation API (innosoftfusiongo.com) only to retrieve your current barcode value. No data is sent anywhere else.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers.
  • No accounts, profiles, or cloud storage operated by the developer.

Data deletion

Use Log out in the app to immediately delete the stored token from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data.

Permissions

  • Internet / Network state — required to retrieve your barcode.

Affiliation

binger barcode is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by UC Riverside or Innosoft. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
